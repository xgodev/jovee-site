How it works

Add the song and play with its band.

01Add the song

Search for it inside the app or bring it from your computer.

02Split the tracks

Drums, bass, vocals, guitar, piano and the rest, each on its own track.

03Set the mix

Level, pan, mute and solo per track: take the guitar out and play in its place.

04Play and see each note

Jam listens to you and marks each note on time, late, wrong or missed.

What it does

Everything you need to play a whole song.

01

Songs and stems

  • Search for the song inside the app or bring your files
  • Drums, bass, vocals, guitar, piano and the rest
  • Split on your computer with built-in models, or online with your MVSEP account
  • Separation passes saved as a preset
  • Stereo split finds each guitar by its place
  • Auto mode
  • Filter by artist and by stem; download the stems
  • On its own or inside Rig and Studio
02

Playback and mix

  • Level, pan, mute and solo per stem
  • Plays on several outputs at once
  • Speed, semitones and A–B loop
  • Finds the tempo; its knob sets the project BPM
  • Share a song by file
03

Tone and markers

  • Each song keeps its own chains and presets
  • Tone points switch chains and mute stems
  • Count-in, spoken section names, tone change and MIDI
  • Several named marker sets
  • Tab sections become markers
  • Alerts on their own output, out of the mix
04

Tab

  • A tab file, or one generated from the guitar stem
  • Refretted for your tuning
  • Several tab windows, one per track
  • Each note marked on time, late, wrong or missed
  • Fix notes by hand, with undo and redo
05

Lyrics and chords

  • Synced lyrics from the tab file, an online library or heard in the vocals
  • Chords over the lyrics, or hidden
  • Text size you choose
  • Chord shapes strip
  • Fix lyric lines by hand
