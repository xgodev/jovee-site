How it works

Add the song and play with its band.

01Add the song

Search for it inside the app or bring it from your computer.

02Split the tracks

Drums, bass, vocals, guitar, piano and the rest, each on its own track.

03Set the mix

Level, pan, mute and solo per track: take the guitar out and play in its place.

04Play and see each note

Jam listens to you and marks each note on time, late, wrong or missed.

What it does

Everything you need to play a whole song.

Scrolling tab

Imported, or generated from the separated guitar, and refretted for your tuning.

Synced lyrics

Lyrics with chords, the line being sung lit up.

Tone per song

Your Rig tone changes by itself at the points of the song you mark.

Markers

A count-in before you come in and each section’s name spoken on time.

Edit tab and lyrics

Fix notes, chords and lyric lines by hand.

Song library

Filter your songs by artist and by the tracks already split.
