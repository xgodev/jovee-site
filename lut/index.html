Guides your guitar setup step by step, measuring each string by its sound.

It says what to play, listens to your guitar and tells you which screw to turn, and which way.

How it works

A setup step by step, listening to each string.

01Play what it asks

The app says which string or note to play.

02It listens

It measures by sound what is out of place.

03Turn the screw

Plain words plus a drawing of the part, the tool and the turn.

04Check it

It listens again until it is right, and shows the before and after.

What it does

A full setup, in the right order.

Tuning

Fresh strings tuned to pitch before anything else.

Neck relief

Measured with a ruler or feeler gauge, with a drawing of where to measure.

String height

How much to raise or lower each bridge saddle.

Pickup height

Which side and how far to turn each pickup screw.

Intonation

Where to move each saddle so fret 12 matches the open string.

Certificate

At the end, a PDF with what each stage found and how it was left.
