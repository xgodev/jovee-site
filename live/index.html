What it does

Everything a rehearsal and a lesson need, in one room.

01

Rooms and invites

  • Create a room with a name and invite the others by code or link
  • Join with the code, then say your name, what you play (guitar, acoustic, bass, voice, keyboard, drums or other) and which chain you send
  • Your recent rooms are listed with their code and how many members they have
  • Two to six musicians, connected straight to each other: the audio never goes through a server
  • If someone drops out, the room tells everyone and reconnects them
  • When you leave, hand the room over to another member or end it for everyone
02

Your sound in the room

  • Your rig’s processed sound is sent, so nobody needs your plugins
  • Choose which chain you send and which input is your talk mic
  • Compressed audio, about 0.5 Mbps of upload each in a five-piece band, or uncompressed
  • The connection runs apart from your rig’s audio and never gets in its way
  • Estimated delay over a wired connection: 8 to 15 ms in the same city, 25 to 35 ms between cities far apart
  • Each member shows their delay and whether they connect directly or through a relay
  • Choose the output where new members play, such as your phones
03

Monitor mix, voice and camera

  • One strip per member, you included, with fader, meter, solo and mute
  • Each member goes to the output you choose: the bass in your phones, the drums in the main speakers
  • Your mix is yours; the others’ mixes don’t change
  • A talk mic to speak with the room, with its own fader beside each member’s instrument, so you balance voice and instrument apart
  • Optional camera, off by default; the video arrives 100 to 200 ms after the audio, and the room’s timing always follows the audio
  • On a weak connection the video loses quality first and the audio stays untouched
04

Playing together

  • One clock for the whole room: the click sounds at the same moment for everyone, and the app warns when yours drifts
  • The song, backing tracks, drums and metronome follow the room’s play, stop and tempo
  • Each machine plays its own copy at full quality; only the commands go over the network
  • Pick what plays in the room and see how many members have it; a song someone doesn’t have is sent to them by the room
  • Whoever creates the room controls play, tempo and recording, and can hand that control to another member
  • JoVee Rig, Studio and Jam join the room from their top bar
05

Recording the rehearsal

  • Each musician records their own track on their own machine, with no network loss
  • The tracks are exchanged afterwards and land aligned on the room’s clock
  • Open the take in JoVee Studio, or show its files
06

Live lessons

  • Teacher and student in the same room, on the same song
  • The teacher sends the exercise, the backing track and their tone, and hears the student play over it
  • The app measures the student’s playing and returns the result to the teacher
  • Recorded lessons, to watch again later
  • Tab and backing tracks in the same place as the lesson
