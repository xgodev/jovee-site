What it does

A complete studio, with your rig inside.

01

Tracks and recording

  • Its own chain on every track, with the Rig's blocks
  • Audio and MIDI tracks
  • Arm, mute, solo, fader and pan
  • Monitor without arming, freeze to save CPU
  • A clean DI in every take, for re-amping
  • Punch-in, loop recording and take comping
  • A count-in of 1 to 4 bars, and a click
  • Stems from JoVee Stem become tracks
02

Clip editing

  • Move, trim, split, copy, paste and duplicate
  • Fade, gain and automatic crossfade
  • Stretch in time without changing the pitch
  • Snap to grid, from whole to sixteenth notes
  • The original file never changes
  • Undo and redo on everything
03

Music and arrangement

  • Type the tempo or tap it
  • Tempo and time signature changes
  • Sections that move everything inside them
  • Chord track
  • Imports Guitar Pro with the tab
  • The same markers as JoVee Jam
04

MIDI

  • Piano roll and drum editor
  • Velocity, controllers and quantize
  • JoVee drum kit or an LV2/VST3 plugin
  • Record from a MIDI keyboard
05

Mixing

  • A live meter on every channel
  • Master with its own chain
  • Aux buses, sends pre or post fader
  • Folder tracks, VCA faders and groups
  • Automation of volume, pan and any knob
  • Plugin latency compensation
  • Its own output for each track
  • Render a track in place or to a new one
  • A color per track, drag to reorder
06

Project and export

  • Save as takes the audio along
  • Autosave
  • Mix as WAV, FLAC or MP3
  • Normalize to -1 dBTP or -14 LUFS
  • 16-bit, 24-bit or 32-bit float, with dither
  • Each track as a separate WAV
07

Control

  • Keyboard shortcuts
  • Mackie Control: transport and 8 channels
  • MIDI learn for transport, faders and knobs
  • MCP and gRPC: all the screen does, from outside
