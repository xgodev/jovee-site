How it works

From the first chord to the band, one step at a time.

01Set your goal

Play with friends, solo, metal, write and record, or go far: the course shows what you need.

02Pick the method

Follow the JoVee School path or the order of recognised music schools.

03Play the lesson

Each lesson has tab, notation, a diagram, audio and the fingering.

04The app measures

Right notes, timing and consistency decide when you pass, with a retest the next day.

What it does

What the course teaches and measures.

101 steps

Eight phases, from the first note to advanced.

Ready for a band

At the end of phase 4 you can play in a band; the rest is specialisation.

Riffs from the start

Power chords come before open chords, so you play riffs early.

Ear and improvising

From the first phase; theory comes once you already play the sound.

Tone, drop D, gallop

Part of the path, for rock and metal.
