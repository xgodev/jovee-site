02Pick the method

What it does

The method of a music school, with the courses you already have.

01

Methods from music schools

  • Rockschool (RSL): grade by grade, in the order of its electric guitar exams
  • Musicians Institute: term by term, in the order of its guitar program in Hollywood
  • Berklee Online: in the order of its guitar courses, from beginner to metal
  • The JoVee School path: the only one that also teaches tone, drop D, gallop and songwriting
  • Every requirement on screen cites the syllabus it came from
  • Switch method at any time: your progress stays, because it is marked on the subject
02

The courses you already bought

  • JoVee School does not sell lessons: it sends you to the right lesson of the course you already have
  • Each step lists the lessons from your courses that teach it, in your teacher's order
  • The lesson plays inside the app, showing only the video
  • See how many steps of the method each course covers, and which steps no course teaches
  • A step that none of your courses teaches gets a public video
03

The map of what you learn

  • Each subject is a dot; a line means one subject opens the next
  • At some points the path splits and you choose: blues, metal or songwriting
  • Subjects outside your goal stay on the map, greyed out, and you can add them
  • Say how much time you have per day and the schedule follows
04

Live lessons with your teacher

  • The class happens inside the app, with JoVee Live: each one on their own rig
  • The teacher sends the exercise, the backing track and their tone to the student
  • The app measures the student and the result goes back to the teacher
  • Tab, backing tracks and recorded classes stay in one place, to go over again
05

The app listens to decide when you move on

  • Right notes, timing, noise, tuning and dynamics, measured from your audio
  • You pass with 4 good takes out of 5, and a retest at least 24 hours later
  • A take that missed comes back with what to fix
  • Each lesson has tab with the fingering, notation, a diagram and audio
