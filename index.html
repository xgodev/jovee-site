Split the chain

Each block's knobs

Your .nam files and IRs

VST3 plugins

Use the VST3 plugins installed on your computer in the middle of the chain.

Hardware pedal in the chain

Send the signal to a pedal on your board and bring it back in the middle of the chain.

Tone Doctor

Shows which block causes fizz, mud or boom and which knob to turn.

Vocal Rasp

A voice block that adds a blues singer's rasp, live.

Blocks for each instrument

Search and install

Your plugins

Edit and go back

Create a plugin

Lyrics with chords

Judge

Markers

Each guitar on its own

Your tuning

Edit by hand

Practice

Play along with drums, metronome, looper and backing tracks without opening another program.

The drums, the metronome and the delays follow the project's tempo.

Drums

Play along with 75 drum grooves and call the fill whenever you want.

Backing tracks

Rock, blues and funk tracks split by part, slower or in another key.

Looper

Up to 8 loops per chain playing together in the same bar, with reverse and waveform trimming.

Metronome

Tap the tempo and pick the time signature and the subdivision.

Harmonics

See the harmonics of each note on a 24-fret neck, with sustain and intonation per note.

Virtual DI

Count-in

Tone per groove

JoVee Ruler

The harmonic field and the scale of any key.

Tools

A tuner for each input and a fader for each output of your interface.

Check tuning, frequencies and levels while you play.

Tuner

One per input: tune one guitar while the other one plays.

Mixer

A fader, mute and solo for each input and output of the interface.

Spectrum

See in real time the frequencies of each input and output.

Meters

The level of each input and output, with the name of each one.

Latency on screen

Measure how long the sound takes from input to output, stage by stage.

Two interfaces

Stage

Compact view

All the chain's knobs on one screen, with its mixer, DI and looper.

MIDI out per scene

Each scene sends PC, CC, note or SysEx to your other gear.

Preset history

MIDI Learn

Bluetooth MIDI

Phone control

Setup wizard

Three looks
