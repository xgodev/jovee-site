Utilities Free

Free utilities

Seven free apps for practice, each on its own.

JoVee Drums

  • Sampled kits
  • 75 grooves in 15 styles
  • A fill when you ask
  • A tone per groove
  • Volume and one or more outputs
  • Import and export kits and grooves
  • From the top bar or the compact view

JoVee Backing

  • Rock, blues and funk tracks in several keys
  • Split by part: solo, rhythm, bass, acoustic
  • Your own files too
  • Speed 0.5× to 2× with the pitch kept
  • ±12 semitones and A–B loop
  • A tone per track
  • Share a track by file
  • Volume and one or more outputs

JoVee Metronome

  • 30 to 300 BPM, with tap
  • Time signature and accent
  • Eighths, triplets and sixteenths
  • Count-in
  • Click, Wood or Beep sound
  • Volume and one or more outputs
  • Drives synced delays and modulations
  • Each preset can carry its BPM

JoVee Tuner

  • One tuner per input
  • As a block anywhere in the chain
  • Or in its own window
  • Opens from Rig, Jam and Studio

JoVee Tab

  • Opens .gp, .gpx, .gp5, .gp4, .gp3 and .gtp
  • Tab and lyrics, track by track
  • Hear the tab
  • Edit fret, string, length and bend
  • Add or delete notes, rename chords
  • Edit lyric lines and their timing
  • Undo and redo
  • Chord shapes

JoVee Lyrics

  • Synced lyrics, the line being sung lit up
  • From the tab file, an online library or heard in the vocals
  • Chords over the lyrics, or hidden
  • Text size you choose
  • Chord shapes strip
  • Fix lyric lines by hand

JoVee Harmonics

  • Harmonics on a keyboard or a 24-fret neck
  • Where on the neck the note was played
  • Note, Hz, string and fret
  • Dissonance, aliasing, sustain, brightness and attack
  • Clashing harmonics drawn in red
  • The note’s spectrum with each harmonic labelled
  • Sustain and intonation for every note you played
  • Read at the input, after any block or at the end of the chain
