What it does

Each instrument with its own rig, and everything to practise and play live around it.

01

Chains

  • One chain per instrument (electric, acoustic, bass, voice, keys or drums), and the block list shows only that instrument's blocks
  • Several instruments on the same interface at once, each one apart from the others
  • Each chain is drawn as a graph: "+" on any wire adds a block right there, and you drag blocks to reorder them
  • Split the signal into as many paths as you want, even inside another path: Split → Mix sums them with level, pan and polarity per path, Split → Y sends each path to its own outputs
  • Insert: send the signal out to a hardware pedal and bring it back in the middle of the chain
  • An output or an input in the middle of the chain, to tap the signal after any block or bring in a second source
  • One chain plays on several outputs, and two interfaces at different sample rates work in the same project
  • Measure the chain's latency from input to output, stage by stage
02

Amps, pedals and effects

  • Built-in amps, preamps and cabs, plus overdrive, distortion, fuzz, compressor, gate, EQ, wah and pitch shifter
  • 14 delays (tape, BBD, ping-pong, granular, pitch…) and 13 reverbs (spring, plate, hall, shimmer…)
  • 16 modulations: chorus, flanger, phaser, tremolo, vibrato, rotary and ring modulator
  • Your own .nam captures and IRs, loaded from any folder
  • LV2 and VST3 plugins in the same chain; a VST3 opens its own window
  • Delays and modulations follow the project tempo by note value: 1/4, dotted 1/8, triplets
  • Every block opens with its own knobs, which you turn while you play; a new block shows its knobs before you add it
  • Vocal Rasp: a voice block that adds a blues singer's rasp, live
03

Presets and scenes

  • Each chain plays a preset from your bank, found by typing its name
  • Up to 8 scenes per preset (verse, chorus, solo), each with its own knob values and blocks on or off, switching with no gap
  • Each scene sends PC, CC, note or SysEx to your other MIDI gear when it loads
  • History: the last 50 versions of each preset with what changed in each, and you bring back the whole preset or a single block
  • Presets are text files you can copy, compare and share
  • Five factory presets made of built-in blocks: clean, crunch, lead, blues and ambient
04

TONE3000 and your plugins

  • Search TONE3000 inside the app by gear and format, and install a NAM capture or an IR in one click with your own key
  • Update a TONE3000 plugin when a newer tone is out, or uninstall it
  • Plugins window: all your plugins, filtered by origin, block type and name
  • Plugin editor: name each capture's parameters as a list, a knob or a switch, and restore any kept version
  • Create a plugin from your own .nam and .wav files
  • Create a modeling: from A2 captures taken in a grid, JoVee trains one NAM whose knobs turn continuously
  • Export a plugin to a single file and import it on another computer
05

Drums, metronome and backing tracks

  • Drum machine: 75 grooves in 15 genres on 2 sampled kits, and a fill whenever you ask
  • One project tempo, nudged or tapped in the top bar: the metronome, the drums and the synced delays follow it
  • Metronome from 30 to 300 BPM, with time signature, subdivision, three click sounds and a count-in
  • Backing tracks by part (solo, rhythm, bass, acoustic), plus your own WAV, FLAC, MP3, OGG or M4A files
  • Play a track slower or faster with the same pitch (0.5× to 2×), in another key (±12 semitones), with an A–B loop
  • Each groove and each track can switch your chains to the preset and scene saved with it
  • Drums, metronome and tracks each play on the outputs you choose
  • JoVee Ruler: the harmonic field and the scale of any key
  • Export a groove or a track to a file and import it on another computer
06

Looper and virtual DI

  • Up to 8 loopers per chain; each new loop locks to the length of the first, and they all restart together
  • Each loop records the dry guitar and keeps the preset it was recorded with, so you solo over it with another tone
  • Per looper: the input it records, the outputs it plays on, level, speed ½×, 1× or 2× and reverse
  • Waveform editor: Fit cuts the take to whole passes of the riff, plus trim, crop, cut, undo and redo
  • A footswitch records, plays, stops and clears the looper of the active chain
  • Save a take to a library every project sees, and export it to a file
  • Virtual DI: a recorded dry guitar (a bundled loop, a saved take or any WAV) loops through the chain while you shape the tone with both hands free
07

Tuner and analysis

  • One tuner per input: tune one guitar while another one plays
  • Spectrum: 63 bands for each output, left and right, in real time
  • Harmonics: each note's harmonics on a 24-fret neck or a keyboard, with sustain, brightness, attack and dissonance
  • Harmonics read after any block of the chain, with sustain and intonation for every note you played
  • Tone Doctor: shows which block causes fizz, mud or boom for the genre you play, and which knob to turn
08

Mixer and meters

  • Mixer: a fader, mute and solo for every input and output of your interfaces
  • Each chain has its own fader on each output it plays, on top of the global one
  • Faders for each chain's volume (up to 200%), its DI and each of its loopers
  • Input and output meters for each chain, named after the interface and its channels
09

Stage and control

  • Compact view: all of a chain's knobs on one screen, with its mixer, DI, looper, Tone Doctor and meters
  • Full-screen mode for touch screens
  • MIDI Learn: press a footswitch or turn a knob and choose what it does
  • A standard MIDI map with 34 commands, ready profiles for known controllers, and Bluetooth MIDI
  • Remote control from a phone or another computer, over the network or Bluetooth, with live meters, tuner, spectrum and harmonics
  • MCP: an AI (Claude, Cursor) builds tones and changes the open rig by conversation
10

Setup and settings

  • A setup wizard on first launch: language, interface, inputs and outputs, TONE3000 key, and a first chain with a riff to hear it
  • Named inputs and outputs, channel by channel, in mono, dual mono or stereo
  • Three looks: Classic, Neon and Rack
  • Nine languages: English, Português, Español, Français, Deutsch, 日本語, 한국어, 中文 and हिन्दी
  • Choose the folder for presets, plugins, takes, backing tracks and drum kits
  • Autosave writes every change to the project
  • One settings file for every JoVee app: change the interface in another app and the open Rig follows
  • Updates from inside the app when a new version comes out
