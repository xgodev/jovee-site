What it does

Each instrument with its own rig.

01

Chains

  • One chain per instrument: guitar, bass, voice, keys or drums
  • Several instruments on one interface at once
  • Add a block on any wire, drag to reorder
  • Split into parallel paths, mixed or sent to their own outputs
  • Insert: out to a real pedal and back
  • Tap or feed the signal mid-chain
  • One chain on several outputs, even across two interfaces
  • Latency measured stage by stage
02

Amps, pedals and effects

  • Built-in amps, cabs, drives, fuzz, EQ and wah
  • 14 delays and 13 reverbs
  • 16 modulations: chorus, flanger, phaser, tremolo…
  • Your own .nam captures and IRs
  • LV2 and VST3 plugins in the same chain
  • Delays and modulations in sync with the tempo
  • Turn any knob while you play
  • Vocal Rasp: a blues rasp on your voice, live
03

Presets and scenes

  • Find a preset by typing its name
  • Up to 8 scenes per preset, switched without gaps
  • Each scene sends MIDI to your other gear
  • History: last 50 versions, restore all or one block
  • Presets are text files you can share
  • Five factory presets, from clean to ambient
04

TONE3000 and your plugins

  • Search TONE3000 and install in one click
  • Update or uninstall TONE3000 tones
  • All your plugins in one window, with filters
  • Plugin editor: name each capture's knobs and switches
  • Build a plugin from your .nam and .wav files
  • Modeling: grid captures become one NAM with continuous knobs
  • Export a plugin to one file
05

Drums, metronome and backing tracks

  • 75 grooves in 15 styles, 2 sampled kits, fills on demand
  • One tempo for click, drums and delays; tap it in
  • Metronome from 30 to 300 BPM, with count-in
  • Backing tracks by part, plus your own audio files
  • Change speed or key, loop A–B
  • A groove or track can switch your preset and scene
  • Drums, click and tracks on the outputs you pick
  • JoVee Ruler: chords and scale of any key
  • Export and import grooves and tracks
06

Looper and virtual DI

  • Up to 8 loopers per chain, always in sync
  • Loops record dry: solo over them with another tone
  • Level, ½× or 2× speed and reverse per looper
  • Waveform editor: Fit, trim, cut, undo
  • Footswitch control of the looper
  • Save takes to a library and export them
  • Virtual DI: a dry guitar loops while you dial in the tone
07

Tuner and analysis

  • One tuner per input
  • 63-band spectrum per output, left and right
  • Harmonics of each note on a 24-fret neck or keyboard
  • Sustain and intonation of every note you play
  • Tone Doctor: which block causes fizz or mud, and which knob to turn
08

Mixer and meters

  • Fader, mute and solo on every input and output
  • Each chain has its own fader per output
  • Faders for chain volume, DI and loopers
  • In and out meters on every chain
09

Stage and control

  • Compact view: a whole chain on one screen
  • Full screen for touch screens
  • MIDI Learn: press a footswitch, pick its job
  • 34 MIDI commands, controller profiles, Bluetooth MIDI
  • Remote control from a phone or another computer
  • MCP: an AI builds tones by chat
10

Setup and settings

  • Setup wizard on first launch
  • Named inputs and outputs, mono or stereo
  • Three looks: Classic, Neon and Rack
  • Nine languages
  • Pick the folder for each kind of file
  • Autosave
  • One settings file for every JoVee app
  • In-app updates
